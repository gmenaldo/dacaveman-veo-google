# DaCaveman — Screenplay

*Four scenes. Comedy. A caveman father and son finally free to be themselves.*

---

## SCENE ONE — The Treehouse

**Setting:** A mountainside. A treehouse embedded in a massive tree, tall windows exposing a living room with vaulted ceilings and chandeliers. Clean wooden floors, luxurious couches, a large flat-screen TV mounted on the wall.

**DAN DACAVEMAN** — strong, chiseled, superior muscular build, five foot five, bright eyes, deep eye sockets, nice face, super long razor-sharp thick ancient toenails and fingernails, thick eyebrows, bearded with a goatee, sharp teeth, superior muscular legs and thighs, covered by a cloth-like material around the waist covering his buttocks and genitals. He is hanging off the side of the mountain, a rope dangling for support. His razor-sharp thick ancient toenails are embedded in the mountainous tree while he works on the finishing touches of the house. He is NOT using the rope to hang on — it only looks like it.

**TOM DACAVEMAN** — another caveman, standing inside the treehouse, staring at his father.

TOM: Come on pop. At least hook the rope to your carabiner, for the illusion of safety.

DAN: You being serious right now?

---

## SCENE TWO — The News

**Setting:** Dan sits in his chair listening to the news. Tom sits in his own chair in pajamas, huge glass of beer, in front of the television. A large fireplace with an old medieval crock pot and a pig on a rotisserie. Dan is on the phone.

NEWS ANNOUNCER (V.O.): This just in — the illegal Alien Ban has been lifted. All aliens in the U.S. and across the globe are considered legal. You are all free to... (pause) be you!

*Video of El Salvadorian, Jamaican, Chinese, Ukrainian, and Russian immigrants across the world dancing in celebration.*

Tom blinks, surprised, watching the dancing on TV.

TOM (on phone): Happy born day pops. What you up to?

DAN (on phone): You know me son, sittin around relaxing.

TOM: Let's go out to eat. Got a birthday surprise for you.

DAN: You know I hate going out in public.

TOM: Get dressed. I'm on my way.

---

## SCENE THREE — The Car Ride

**Setting:** Tom arrives at his father's house and honks. Dan comes out dressed for winter — heavy coat, hat, boots, gloves, scarf. His hair grows out of the hat. Beard is long. He hops in the car.

TOM: We ain't been out.

DAN: You know I hate going out in public.

TOM: Pop, I wouldn't ask you to go out if it wasn't ok.

*Dan looks serious.*

DAN: So, you mean, it passed?

TOM: Everyone's excited!

DAN: They have every reason to be.

TOM: To celebrate, how bout a mani-pedi?

DAN: Always wondered about those. What do they do?

TOM: Well, two good looking women work on your feet and hands at the same time while massaging your calf muscles.

DAN: Sounds good. I'm overdue to be pampered. Then, can we eat?

TOM: Just sit back and enjoy the show.

*They arrive at the shop.*

---

## SCENE FOUR — The Spa

**Setting:** Inside the spa. Wide picture window with leather chairs in front of it that you have to step up on, with a spa for the feet and water running. Other chairs on the lower level. A display of nail tips in various colors. A TV showing the government's decision to release the Illegal Alien Act. Two guests in shorts and t-shirts. A lady at the counter in a blue nurse outfit, short dark medium-long hair.

SHOP WORKER: Hello. Can I get the name for the appointment?

TOM: Tom, and Dan DaCaveman.

SHOP WORKER: Have a seat right here.

*They are directed to the picture-window chairs. People begin to gather around the window outside, looking in.*

DAN: Wow, son! I feel good.

TOM: Told you. This one of the best feelings in the world. To have women rub and pamper you.

*Tom looks at his dad.*

TOM: Be comfortable. We don't have to hide anymore. It's the law.

DAN: Everything?

TOM: Everything, pop. Watch me!

*Tom breathes deeply and transforms into his normal self — sackcloth covering his chest like a tank top, waist to his upper thighs, feet and hand nails exposed. People gasp.*

*Dan takes a deep breath and transforms — sackcloth, exposing most of his body, chest hair, covering only his waist down to his upper thighs. People outside run to the windows in a panic. People take videos and pictures.*

DAN: Ah, it's good to finally give my feet some air!

TOM: You could've left some clothing on.

*Two guests get up and run out. One person throws up a little in their mouth.*

EMPLOYEE ONE: How are you guys doing — ohh my God!

*Employee One jumps back. Employee Two has a look of fear and confusion.*

EMPLOYEE TWO: What the hell am I looking at?

*Employee One panics, walking in a circle.*

EMPLOYEE ONE: We gotta close the shop! We gotta close the shop!

*The owner runs out in haste, almost falling.*

OWNER: Are you freakin kidding me! The ban hasn't even been lifted for twenty-four hours yet. I thought you guys only come out at night!

DAN: I know that song! The freaks come out at night! Nope, me and my son here for the free mani-pedi. It's my birthday.

*The owner looks in disgust, takes deep gulps.*

OWNER: That's nasty.

*The owner pulls out a bullhorn and yells to the back of the shop.*

OWNER: Get me a chisel, a sander and a hazmat bucket. Better make it two hazmat buckets, a few face shields and some thick rubber gloves!

EMPLOYEE ONE: Naw man! One of y'all gonna have to pay!

OWNER: You can't say that!

EMPLOYEE ONE: What you mean? We can close the shop for the week on this ticket alone!

*Scene ends with Employee One and Employee Two working on their nails, showing discomfort, while the Owner checks in another woman. She sees the two cavemen, takes a deep breath, and transforms into herself as a cavewoman — sackcloth covering her chest and her waist down to the middle of her thighs, nails on hands and feet the same as Tom and Dan's.*

FEMALE CAVEWOMAN: It's my birthday!

---

*End of screenplay.*
