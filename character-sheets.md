## DAN DACAVEMAN (Father)

- Strong, chiseled, superior muscular build. Five foot five.
- Bright eyes, deep eye sockets, nice face.
- Super long, razor-sharp, thick, ancient toenails and fingernails.
- Thick eyebrows. Bearded with a goatee. Sharp teeth.
- Superior muscular legs and thighs.
- Wardrobe: cloth-like material around the waist covering buttocks and genitals. Winter gear in Scene Three (heavy coat, hat, boots, gloves, scarf — hair growing out of the hat, long beard). Sackcloth in Scene Four (chest hair exposed, waist to upper thighs).
- Signature: the ancient nails. Always visible. Never trimmed.

## TOM DACAVEMAN (Son)

- Younger caveman, Dan's son. Similar build, slightly leaner.
- Same ancient razor-sharp thick nails on hands and feet — nails as long as his fingers on hands, prehistorically thick on feet.
- Wardrobe: pajamas in Scene Two. Sackcloth in Scene Four (chest like a tank top, waist to upper thighs).
- Signature: the nails. Same prehistoric thickness as his father.

## Reference image recipe (for Nano Banana / Imagen)

For each character, generate three images on a plain background:
1. Clean head-and-shoulders portrait.
2. Three-quarter view showing wardrobe and proportions.
3. Full body showing the nails clearly.

All three must show the same identity, age, hairstyle, and visual style. These become your Veo "ingredients."
